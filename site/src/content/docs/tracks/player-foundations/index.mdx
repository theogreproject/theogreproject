## What you’ll learn

- Turning “backstory” into **play hooks**
- Making characters that collaborate (without forced drama)

## What you’ll ship

- A set of **Character Flags** (what you want the GM/table to hit)
- A **Party Bond** artifact (why this group actually works)
- A session-ready **hook kit** for your PC
