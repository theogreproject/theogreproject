Welcome, traveler! The Ogre Project is a **learn-by-doing curriculum** for **TTRPG players and Game Masters***.

You'll move through short lessons, focused exercises, and small projects that produce usable table material: sessions, adventures, tools, and settings.

## Who this is for

- **New to TTRPGs**: learn how to play well and support the table.
- **New GMs**: run your first sessions with confidence.
- **Experienced GMs**: tighten your method, prep smarter, and design better adventures.

## How to use this site

1. Read one lesson at a time.
2. Do your exercise immediately (even if it feels small).
3. Save your work somewhere (notes repo, Notion, Google Doc -- anything!).
4. Build toward the module project.

:::tip
Progress beats perfection. The goal is playable output, not "the best possible lore document."
:::

Write a short paragraph answering:

- What kind of TTRPG experience do you want to create at your table?
- What do you struggle with most right now (prep, improv, pacing, rules, worldbuilding...)?

Save it. You'll revisit this later.
