## The quest loop

<Steps>

1. **Read** a focused lesson (short, actionable, table-minded)

2. **Practice** with an exercise that forces decisions.

3. **Ship** a deliverable you can actually use: a scene, a hook, a faction, a handout, a prep pack, and more!

</Steps>
